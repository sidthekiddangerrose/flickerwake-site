Flickerwake

Thirty years ago, Sera climbed a storm-wrapped ridge to confront Varos of the Spirals. She never returned. Now the Ember Rings are going dark, and her son Alden must cross a dying world to uncover what happened.

Flickerwake is a single-player, illustrated dark fantasy RPG told through text-driven exploration, voiced cinematics and the people you meet on the road to the ridge. Turn-based battles, decided on a timing bar, stand between Alden and the ridge.

About the game

Explore more than 25 interconnected locations, one illustrated, text-driven scene at a time, from the ruins of a hunter's camp to ancient graveyards and the storm-shrouded ridge. Use LOOK to search each scene; what you find changes from one new game to the next. Your map and journal fill in as the world reveals its secrets.

The Ember Flame you carry is a resource as well as a symbol of hope. Travel can consume it, while resting, retreating and defeat take their toll. Keep it burning and your strikes land harder; let it fade and enemies hit harder.

Learn each enemy's weakness and record it in your bestiary. Pair a primary rune with a secondary rune for 12 different ways to fight. New Game+ brings tougher enemies and a new path with more story and a new location.

How it was made

Flickerwake is written, designed and built by one developer. Some art, cutscenes and a little of the voice work were made with AI tools, then chosen and edited by the developer. The gameplay, story and design are his own.
