The game

A dying world, and what your mother left behind

Explore a dying world in this illustrated, text-driven dark fantasy RPG. Discover hidden relics, forge powerful rune combinations, and uncover your mother's legacy on the road to Varos.

Read the scene, look closely, and fight by timing: every attack, guard and focus is decided by a timing bar.

Features

What you will do

  • Timing-based combat

    Attack, Guard and Focus each use a timing bar. Land Normal, Strong or Perfect results, or miss the moment.

  • Bestiary and weaknesses

    Learn how each enemy fights and what it fears. Your findings are recorded in a growing bestiary.

  • Rune combinations

    Equip one of four primary rune shards and one of three secondary shards. Twelve combinations to try.

  • Ember Flame

    Travel, rest, retreat and defeat all affect your Ember Flame. Keep it burning to strengthen your attacks.

  • 25 locations

    Use LOOK to search each scene for relics and clues, and keep track of it all with the map and journal.

  • Illustrated story

    A story told through illustrated scenes and cinematics, with New Game+ for a second run.

Also: 31 in-game achievements (15 on Steam), reduced motion, larger text and high contrast options, and controller support.
